=== SYSTEM ===
You are an ecologist who annotates scientific studies for a German literature database
on biodiversity. In this task you decide only one field: biodiversity_level, the levels
of biodiversity that THIS document examines. You prove every answer with an exact quote
from the text you are given.

=== USER ===
## Task
Read the text below (part {chunk_id} of document {document_id}) and decide which levels of
biodiversity it examines. Work through the steps first, then give the labels.

## The levels (copy the value character by character)
Artenvielfalt
    How many species there are: species richness, number of species, diversity indices
    (Shannon, Simpson), species lists or inventories of an area, species gains and losses.
    The most frequent level: about two thirds of the labelled documents have it.
Zusammensetzung der Artengemeinschaft
    Which species occur together and in which proportions: community composition,
    assemblages, abundance or biomass of several species compared between sites,
    treatments or years, community shifts, microbial communities from sequencing.
    About one third of the labelled documents.
Habitatdiversität
    The variety of habitats: several habitat or biotope types compared, habitat
    heterogeneity of a landscape, the state or loss of habitats, habitat mosaics.
    About one in six labelled documents.
Funktionelle Diversität
    Traits and functions: functional groups, functional traits, functional diversity
    indices, multifunctionality, effects of diversity on ecosystem functions.
    About one in six labelled documents.
Genetische Diversität
    Variation within species: genetic variation within or between populations,
    genotypes, varieties or breeds, molecular markers, population genetics.
    About one in fifteen labelled documents.
Strukturelle Diversität
    Physical structure: stand or vegetation structure, layers, deadwood, mixed versus
    pure stands, structural heterogeneity of habitats. About one in fifteen.
Phylogenetische Diversität
    Relatedness of species: phylogenetic diversity or relatedness indices, phylogenetic
    trees of a community. Rare, and almost always together with other levels.

## Rules
L1  A level is labelled when THIS document examines biodiversity at this level: it
    measures, compares, models or reviews it as a main subject. A level that appears only
    as background, as a goal in the introduction or in citations of other work is not
    labelled.
L2  About four in ten documents get no level at all, e.g. documents about the physiology,
    growth or behaviour of organisms without comparing species, about soil, water or
    climate processes, or about policy, management or services without biodiversity data.
    An empty list is a valid answer.
L3  A document may have several levels. Check every level on its own; do not stop after
    the first one. Typical pairs: Artenvielfalt with Zusammensetzung der Artengemeinschaft;
    Artenvielfalt with Habitatdiversität; Artenvielfalt with Funktionelle Diversität.
L4  Confidence. For every level, estimate the probability (0.0 to 1.0) that the database
    gives THIS document this level. Give a label for every level whose probability is at
    least 0.2, with its probability in "confidence". Use the whole range honestly:
    0.9 = clearly a main subject, 0.5 = could go either way, 0.2 = possible but doubtful.
L5  Every label needs a "quote": a verbatim passage from the text below, copied character
    by character, 5 to 40 words, with no "..." and no paraphrase.
L6  Answer with one JSON object only, exactly in the structure below. No markdown, no code
    fences, no text before or after the JSON.

## Steps (write them into "reasoning", short notes, max. 100 words)
S1  What does THIS document itself examine (its data, methods and results)?
S2  For each of the seven levels: is it examined here (rule L1)? How likely would the
    database label it?
S3  Self-check: every value copied from the list, every quote exact, every confidence
    at least 0.2.
